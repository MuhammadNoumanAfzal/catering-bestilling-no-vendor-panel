1. OFFER STATE AND PERMISSIONS
WAITING_FOR_VENDOR: vendor can send a quote or reject the request.
OFFERED: customer can accept or decline; vendor can change/withdraw the quote or
reject the request. Vendor UI says Offer sent; customer UI says Offer received.
ACCEPTED: hide/deny decline, withdrawal and quote edits. Customer can continue to
normal checkout. Acceptance is not payment/order confirmation.
DECLINED: terminal. Deny messages, quoting, accepting and checkout for this request.
WITHDRAWN: current quote is inactive; deny acceptance/checkout of that quote.
Request/chat may stay open and vendor may send a new quote. Preserve historical
quote/events; next quote gets a NEW quote revision.
EXPIRED/CLOSED/ORDERED: enforce the respective terminal/payment rules server-side.
Every change increments request revision and updates permissions atomically.
Reject stale expectedRevision, stale quoteRevision, unauthorized actors and
invalid/expired prices without partially committing state or outbound events.
